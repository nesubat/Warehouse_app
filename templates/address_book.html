🚚 Courier services

The services offered in the consignment preview and written in the courier CSV. Services in a group share its logo: Stitch Labels puts it on each label beside LABEL 1 OF 2, picked by the service in the courier CSV. Logo: a strip about 6.3 times as wide as it is tall (e.g. 1000 × 160 px PNG): it prints as tall as the LABEL / PAGE boxes. Move a service to another group and its labels get that group's logo.
