🧵 Stitch Labels

Puts each courier label from the courier portal's label PDF onto its packing label, in the courier label region. Each courier label is matched by the unique code in its Item Ref / Item Reference (e.g. 01-QWXK-L-1-Store), so the order the portal prints them in doesn't matter. The PDF starts with a report, then the packing labels with their courier label, then those without one; courier labels that matched nothing are added 4-up at the end, in cut-and-stack order. Labels are placed as sharp images (600 dpi) so barcodes and QR codes still scan; portrait and landscape labels both fit.
