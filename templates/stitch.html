🧵 Stitch Labels

Puts each courier label from the courier portal's label PDF onto its packing label, in the courier label region. Every courier label is read first and matched by the unique code in its Item Ref / Item Reference (e.g. 01-QWXK-L-1-Store), so the order the portal prints them in doesn't matter; a pack of several boxes (2 x OB170170170) takes one courier label per box. When everything matches, the Complete Labels PDF is made straight away. When something doesn't, you go straight to matching by hand, and the PDF is made once, when you press Finalise. Labels are placed as sharp images (600 dpi) so barcodes and QR codes still scan; portrait and landscape labels both fit.
